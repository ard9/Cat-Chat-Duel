    How to play

    1. Tap QUICK MATCH to join 1 to 5 random players, or create a room and send the 4-letter code to friends.
    2. Everyone gets the same word at the same time. Type it before the timer runs out.
    3. Finish in time and your worker cuts the branch. The faster you are, the more points you get.
    4. Too slow, a wrong key, or last of the whole group? Your branch drops closer to your cat.
    5. When a branch lands, that player is out. The last cat standing wins!

    If several players make it through level 10, the highest score wins. Lost your connection? You have 15 seconds to come back; the match waits for you.
